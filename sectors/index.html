Sectors We Serve

From a single school to a multi-academy trust or commercial landlord, we understand the facilities challenges specific to your sector.

🏫

Schools & Academies

Compliance audits, premises management and holiday works planning tailored to individual schools, plus competent person support for schools without a dedicated H&S lead. If you expect 200+ people on site at events, Martyn's Law applies from spring 2027, and the Crime and Policing Act 2026 changes how you manage volunteers and visitors from this academic year. Read our Martyn's Law guide →

🏛️

Multi-Academy Trusts

Consistent facilities standards and compliance oversight across multiple sites, plus governance-level support as accountability expectations on trusts continue to rise. Read about the Hillsborough Law →

🏠

Community Centres

Practical support for venues balancing tight budgets with public safety obligations — including standard-tier Martyn's Law readiness for any venue that can hit 200+ visitors at an event, plus competent person cover for day-to-day risks a volunteer-run venue can't always manage in-house. Read our Martyn's Law guide →

⚽

Sports Clubs

Facilities and compliance support for clubs managing pitches, pavilions and clubhouses — including Martyn's Law standard-tier readiness for clubhouses and matchdays that draw a crowd, and competent person cover for equipment, changing rooms and matchday risk. Read our Martyn's Law guide →

🏢

Commercial Landlords

FM reviews, contractor management and compliance audits across your portfolio. If any of your buildings include residential space, the Fire Safety (Residential Evacuation Plans) Regulations 2025 now require person-centred evacuation plans for residents who'd need help. Read our guide →

🔑

Housing Associations

Housing associations and local authority landlords are managing a fast-growing compliance workload — Awaab's Law Phase 2 (from 30 November 2026) extends fixed repair-response deadlines well beyond damp and mould, covering excess cold and heat, structural issues, electrical hazards and fire risk. We support housing providers with compliance audits, repairs-process reviews and interim FM cover to keep pace with these deadlines without overloading in-house teams. Read our Awaab's Law guide →

🏭

Industrial Units

Statutory maintenance and compliance oversight for industrial and warehouse space, including getting ahead of mandatory digital waste tracking from October 2026, plus competent person support for the manual handling, work equipment and COSHH risks these sites carry day to day. Read our waste tracking guide →

💜

Charities & Third Sector

Cost-conscious FM support that respects a charity's budget and mission — including getting audit-ready ahead of a funder or insurer visit, and ongoing competent person support for charities without the budget for an in-house H&S manager. Read our audit-readiness checklist →

💼

SMEs

Facilities support that scales with you, without the cost of an in-house FM manager — including acting as your external competent person for health & safety, a legal duty every business has to meet. Read about Health & Safety Consultancy →

🏋️

Gyms & Leisure Facilities

Health clubs, leisure centres and swimming pools carry legionella risk from showers, pools and plant rooms under ACOP L8, on top of routine equipment inspection and PPM duty. Busier sites with 200+ visitors at peak times or events fall into Martyn's Law standard tier from spring 2027. We provide competent person support and compliance audits that keep pace with member numbers. Read about Health & Safety Consultancy →

🏨

Independent Hotels

Guest sleeping accommodation carries a higher fire-risk category, and legionella control under ACOP L8 matters wherever water sits in guest-room systems and function-space plant. Hotels with 200+ capacity across event or conference space fall into Martyn's Law standard tier from spring 2027. We help independent hotels and small groups get compliance audits and competent person support in place without a head-office H&S team. Read our Martyn's Law guide →

🍽️

Independent Restaurants

Kitchen extraction servicing, gas safety certification and COSHH-controlled cleaning chemicals are the facilities risks that sit alongside food hygiene — and the ones a busy independent restaurant or small group is most likely to let slip. We handle the facilities and fire-safety side, plus competent person support, so you can focus on the kitchen. Read about Health & Safety Consultancy →

🏡

Care Homes

Evacuating residents with mobility or cognitive needs is the defining fire-safety challenge for care premises — the Fire Safety (Residential Evacuation Plans) Regulations 2025 now require person-centred evacuation plans for exactly this. Add legionella control across en-suite and communal water systems and routine statutory maintenance, and the facilities workload sits alongside CQC's clinical oversight, not instead of it. We support the premises and compliance side. Read our guide →

🦺

Nurseries & Early Years

Fire safety, COSHH-controlled cleaning products and playground equipment inspection all need managing under the Early Years Foundation Stage framework, often without any dedicated H&S resource on site. We act as external competent person under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, plus policy and risk assessment support sized for a single setting or a small group. Read about Health & Safety Consultancy →

🕊️

Faith & Community Venues

Under Martyn's Law, places of worship sit in the standard tier regardless of size — a different threshold to other community venues. We help churches, mosques, temples and other faith venues get standard-tier readiness in place, alongside the same competent person and compliance support we provide to volunteer-run community buildings. Read our Martyn's Law guide →
