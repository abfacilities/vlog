Soft Services & FM Support

Cleaning, grounds, pest control, security and FM support services — all managed to agreed specifications so your building looks right, functions well and your occupiers are satisfied.

What We Manage

Soft Services Managed to Specification

Soft services set the standard of your building. Poorly managed cleaning, grounds or pest control affects occupier satisfaction and your reputation. We manage these contracts on your behalf — to agreed KPIs, with regular reviews and clear accountability.

🧹

Cleaning

Daily, weekly and periodic cleaning contracts managed to specification. Supervision, quality checks and contractor performance reviewed regularly.

🌿

Grounds Maintenance

External areas, landscaping, car parks and perimeter maintained to agreed schedule. Winter gritting and snow clearance management included.

🐀

Pest Control

Pest control contracts managed, baiting programmes maintained and reactive treatment arranged promptly. Full audit documentation retained.

Waste Management

Security
