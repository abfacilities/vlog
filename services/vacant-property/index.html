Vacant Property Inspections

Regular, documented inspections for commercial landlords, managing agents and portfolio operators. Straightforward inspections from £125; larger premises and recurring schedules quoted individually.

From £125 per inspection

Starting rate for straightforward inspections within an agreed service area and scope. Larger premises, specialist requirements and additional travel are quoted individually.

Vacant commercial properties need regular, documented inspections to protect your asset, maintain your insurance position and demonstrate that the property is being properly managed. AB Facilities Consultants provides professional inspection visits with a written report following each visit.

Who this service is for

  • Commercial landlords with vacant units
  • Managing agents overseeing vacant properties
  • Property owners and portfolio operators
  • Businesses with temporarily unoccupied premises

What each inspection covers

  • Security — doors, windows, perimeter, access points
  • Visible signs of unauthorised access or occupation
  • Water ingress and damp evidence
  • General condition of the building fabric
  • Utility isolation checks (where agreed)
  • External condition and boundary
  • Photographic evidence of key findings
  • Written inspection report issued after each visit

Inspection frequency

Inspection frequency should follow your insurance policy requirements and the agreed inspection scope. Recurring schedules — weekly or other frequencies — are quoted separately. We will confirm the agreed frequency in writing before inspections begin.

Important: Vacant property inspections do not automatically satisfy every insurance policy condition. You should confirm your specific policy requirements with your insurer. ABFC inspection reports document the condition of the property at the time of each visit; they are not a guarantee of insurance compliance.

Remedial works

Any remedial work identified during an inspection must be separately approved and quoted before it is carried out. Inspections and remedial works are priced independently.

Standalone or as part of FM

Vacant property inspections can be purchased on a standalone basis or incorporated into a bespoke managed FM contract.
