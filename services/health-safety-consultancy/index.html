Health & Safety Consultancy

IOSH-backed health & safety support — policies, risk assessments and competent person cover for organisations that don't have (or don't need) a full-time H&S manager.

From £350 per engagement

Under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, every organisation must appoint a "competent person" to help meet its health and safety duties. For many schools, SMEs and community organisations, bringing that in-house isn't practical — that's where we come in. As an IOSH member, Adam provides that competent person support directly, backed by the same practical, no-nonsense approach behind our compliance audit work.

What's included

  • Health & safety policy development and review
  • Risk assessments beyond fire — manual handling, COSHH, display screen equipment, work equipment and general workplace hazards
  • Acting as your external competent person under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999
  • Accident and incident investigation, including RIDDOR reporting support
  • Method statements and risk assessments (RAMS) for contractor and site work
  • H&S training needs analysis and toolbox talk coordination

How this differs from a Compliance Audit

Our Compliance Audits are a point-in-time review of fire safety, statutory maintenance and premises H&S. Health & Safety Consultancy is the ongoing version — ourselves acting as your named competent person, keeping policies and risk assessments current, and being on hand when an incident happens or a new activity needs assessing. Many clients use both: an audit to establish the baseline, then ongoing consultancy to keep it current.

Available as a one-off engagement or folded into our Core, Plus and Enhanced monthly retainer packages from £650/month.
