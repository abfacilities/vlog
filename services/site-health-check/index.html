FM Site Health Check

A structured, one-off assessment of how your facilities management is working — what's in place, what's missing and what to do about it. From £495.

From £495

Starting fee for a single-site assessment. Additional sites, complex premises and specialist inspection requirements are quoted separately. Scope and fee confirmed before attendance.

Many organisations don't have a clear picture of their FM arrangements — which contracts are in place, when they expire, what's missing, and whether the right maintenance activities are actually happening. The FM Site Health Check gives you that picture in a single engagement.

It's a practical assessment, not an audit. ABFC will review your current FM arrangements, speak with relevant people on site and produce a written report identifying what is working, what needs attention and recommended next steps.

What the assessment covers

  • Current FM arrangements and contract position
  • Planned and reactive maintenance activity
  • Contractor management and documentation
  • Compliance administration (what records exist and what's missing)
  • Procurement and value for money
  • Recommended priorities and next steps

Important — scope of this service: The FM Site Health Check is an initial FM management assessment only. It is not a fire risk assessment, an asbestos survey, a legionella risk assessment, a statutory compliance audit, or any other specialist technical inspection. It does not constitute certification of compliance with any regulation or standard. Where specialist assessments are required, we can advise on appropriate next steps, but those are separate engagements.

How it works

  1. Get in touch to discuss your site, size and any known issues
  2. ABFC confirms the scope and fee in writing before attendance
  3. Site visit and document review carried out
  4. Written report delivered with findings and prioritised recommendations
  5. Optional follow-up call to discuss findings

Who this is for

  • Organisations without an FM manager who want to understand their position
  • New building managers or estates leads taking over responsibility
  • Businesses that have grown but whose FM arrangements haven't kept pace
  • Organisations preparing for a lease renewal, a move or a change of contractor

Following a health check, some clients move on to an FM Review, Interim FM support or a monthly FM package.
