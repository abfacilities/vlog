Compliance Audits

Fire safety, statutory maintenance and H&S inspections that catch problems before a regulator does.

From £350 per site

Our compliance audits give you a clear, honest picture of where your site stands against fire safety, statutory maintenance and health & safety obligations — plus a prioritised list of what to fix first.

What's included

  • Fire risk assessment review and gap analysis
  • Statutory maintenance and testing schedule review (gas, electrical, water hygiene, lifts, fire equipment)
  • Contractor compliance and certification checks
  • Full health & safety walk-round inspection
  • Written report with prioritised findings and an action plan

New in 2026/27

Alongside our core fire safety, statutory maintenance and H&S inspection work, our compliance audits now also cover:

  • Martyn's Law readiness reviews — for schools, academies, community centres and sports clubs that may need to meet standard-tier duties under the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025 from spring 2027. We assess your typical visitor numbers, review your procedures against the Home Office's statutory guidance, and help build staff training into your existing compliance calendar. Read our full guide →
  • Awaab's Law hazard-response reviews — for housing associations and local authority landlords preparing for Phase 2 (from 30 November 2026), which extends fixed repair-response deadlines to excess cold/heat, structural issues, electrical hazards, fire and more. We review your repairs triage, contractor SLAs and record-keeping against the statutory deadlines. Read our full guide →
