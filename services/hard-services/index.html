Compliance

Statutory Compliance Managed End-to-End

Every building has statutory compliance obligations. We track every certificate, every inspection date and every remedial action — nothing lapses, nothing is missed.

Electrical — EICR

Electrical Installation Condition Reports managed to 5-year cycle (or as required). Remedials tracked and completed before certificates are cleared.

Fire — BS 5839 / BS 5306

Fire alarm, emergency lighting, suppression and extinguisher maintenance to current British Standards. Fire risk assessment liaison.

Water — L8 Legionella

Legionella risk assessments, written schemes, temperature monitoring, chlorination and all ACoP L8 obligations managed and documented.

Gas — CP17 / CGAS

Commercial gas safety inspections, boiler servicing and gas system maintenance to Gas Safe requirements.

HVAC — F-Gas

F-Gas compliance for air conditioning and refrigeration systems. Qualified engineer servicing and leak testing as required.

Lifting — LOLER

LOLER thorough examinations for all lifting equipment. Certificates retained and renewal dates tracked automatically.
