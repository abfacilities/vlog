Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Independent Hotels

Guest Experience Protection

Maintenance failures are immediately visible to guests — and immediately shared in reviews. Proactive FM management reduces the risk of issues arising during occupied periods.

Complex Building Systems

Hotels operate complex M&E systems — HVAC, plumbing, lifts, kitchen extraction, fire detection and suppression — all requiring regular servicing and careful management.

Statutory Compliance

Fire safety, gas, electrical, legionella, lift safety and food safety premises obligations all apply. Missing an inspection or renewal creates regulatory and insurance risk.

Contractor Disruption to Guests

Maintenance works in an occupied hotel must be carefully managed to avoid disrupting guests. Without coordination, contractors arriving unannounced or working in public areas creates complaints.

Seasonal Demand Pressures

Many independent hotels have high and low seasons. Low occupancy periods should be used productively for planned maintenance — but without FM coordination, this rarely happens systematically.

Owner-Operator Capacity

Independent hotel owners are often managing front-of-house operations, staff and revenue. Premises management can be neglected simply because there is no capacity to focus on it properly.

FAQs

Common Questions

What statutory inspections does a hotel require?

Fire risk assessment (annually), gas safety certificate (annually), EICR (every 5 years for most commercial buildings), legionella risk assessment, lift inspection (every 6 months), emergency lighting tests, fire detection servicing and kitchen extract cleaning. We track all of these.

Can maintenance be managed around room occupancy?

Yes — we coordinate all contractor visits around room availability and guest schedules, with public area works planned for low-occupancy periods.

Do you coordinate kitchen equipment maintenance?

Yes — we manage kitchen equipment servicing, extraction system cleaning (a fire safety requirement) and commercial kitchen maintenance with appropriately qualified contractors.
