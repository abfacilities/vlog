FM for Commercial Offices

Office buildings and business parks need reliable, professional FM management. We handle the full facilities function so your building runs well and your tenants or staff aren't chasing problems.

What We Manage

Keeping Your Office Building Running

Office FM involves managing a wide range of contractors, compliance obligations and day-to-day requests — all of which take time away from running your business or managing your portfolio. We act as your dedicated FM manager, handling everything from planned maintenance and compliance to reactive repairs and contractor management.

Planned Maintenance & Compliance

EICR, fire systems, L8, HVAC and all statutory inspections managed and documented.

Reactive Maintenance

Single point of contact for reactive issues — faults reported, contractors despatched, works completed.

Cleaning & Grounds

Cleaning and grounds maintenance contracts managed to agreed specifications.

Tenant & Occupier Liaison

Managing communication with occupiers, responding to requests and escalating where needed.

Monthly Reporting

Regular written reports covering maintenance activity, compliance status and open items.

Contractor Management

All contractors sourced, instructed and managed by us — with full audit trail.

Common Problems

What Office Clients Usually Come to Us With

Compliance Gaps Nobody Is Tracking

Certificates have lapsed, PPM hasn't been done, or nobody is sure what the current status is across the building.

Too Many Contractors, No Accountability

Multiple contractors, no oversight, reactive rather than planned — and nobody managing them or recording what's been done.

Tenant Complaints Going Unresolved

Tenants reporting the same issues repeatedly because there's no structured process for receiving and closing them out.

An FM Manager Who Left

The person who managed the building has gone and there's no plan for who does it now — compliance and maintenance are starting to slip.

Costs That Keep Rising

FM spend has drifted upward with no clear picture of where the money is going or whether the value is there.

Too Small to Justify In-House FM

One or two buildings — not enough to warrant a full-time FM manager, but too much to manage on the side of another role.
