Understanding the Sector

Facilities Challenges for Community Centres

Volunteer Committee Management

Many community centres are managed by volunteer committees without premises management expertise. Statutory obligations can be missed simply through lack of awareness.

Aging Building Stock

Community buildings are often older structures with aging electrical, heating and roofing systems — requiring more reactive maintenance and more careful compliance management.

Public Liability and Compliance

Community centres open to the public carry significant liability. Fire safety, asbestos management, legionella and electrical safety obligations apply regardless of charitable status.

Limited Maintenance Budgets

With tight budgets, every maintenance pound needs to work harder. Unplanned reactive repairs are more expensive than planned preventative maintenance — but without FM structure, PPM rarely happens.

Multi-Use Hireable Spaces

Centres used by multiple hirers for different activities create ongoing responsibility for ensuring the premises are safe and compliant at all times.

Grant Compliance

Centres funded by grants or public bodies may have premises management requirements written into funding conditions — documentation and reporting obligations that require FM input.

FAQs

Common Questions

We're a registered charity — do your services apply to us?

Yes. Charitable status doesn't remove premises safety obligations. Fire safety, electrical and gas compliance apply equally — and many grant funders now require evidence of proper FM management.

Our committee has no FM expertise — where do we start?

A premises compliance audit is usually the best starting point. It gives you a clear picture of your current position and a prioritised action plan — without overwhelming the committee.

Can you work within a limited budget?

Yes. We'll prioritise the most critical statutory requirements first and help you plan maintenance activity in a way that fits your budget and avoids expensive emergency repairs.
