Understanding the Sector

The Facilities Challenges Schools Face

Statutory Compliance Obligations

Schools must manage fire risk assessments, asbestos registers, legionella risk assessments, electrical installation condition reports, gas safety and more. Missing an inspection or renewal can expose the school to enforcement action and reputational damage.

Term-Time Disruption

Most significant maintenance work must be planned around school holidays. Without a properly coordinated PPM schedule, reactive jobs pile up during term time — disrupting lessons and creating safety risks.

Contractor Management

Schools have a duty to manage contractor safety on site. Verifying insurance, competency and safeguarding requirements for every trade contractor is time-consuming and rarely done consistently without an FM structure in place.

Site Manager Capacity

Many schools rely on a single site manager to cover all maintenance, compliance and contractor coordination. Without senior FM support, gaps appear — particularly during staff absence or when multiple issues arise simultaneously.

Budget Transparency

School governors and trusts need clear visibility of premises spending against budget. Without structured reporting, FM costs are hard to forecast, justify or challenge.

Safeguarding on Site

Any contractor working on school premises must comply with safeguarding policies. Managing this alongside day-to-day maintenance coordination adds another layer of administrative responsibility for school leadership.

FAQs

Common Questions from Schools

Do we need a formal FM provider if we have a site manager?

A site manager handles day-to-day tasks but rarely has capacity to manage strategic compliance planning, contractor vetting and board-level reporting. FM consultancy sits alongside your existing team — it doesn't replace them.

How often do statutory inspections need to happen?

It depends on the system. Fire risk assessments: at least annually (more frequently after changes to the building or use). EICRs: every 5 years for most school buildings. Gas safety: annually. Legionella risk assessments: reviewed regularly, with water temperature monitoring ongoing. We track all of these for you.

Can you manage contractor safeguarding compliance?

We can manage the process of checking that contractors working on your site have appropriate safeguarding policies and that your visitor/contractor procedures are followed. DBS checks for individual workers are the contractor's own responsibility — we ensure your process for managing this is consistent.

What documentation do we receive?

Monthly reports covering all maintenance activity, compliance certificate status, contractor work completed and outstanding items. Documents are provided in a format suitable for governing body meetings and trust board reporting.

Do you work with multi-academy trusts?

Yes — we offer coordinated FM across multiple school sites with central reporting for the trust. See our Multi-Academy Trust page for more detail.
