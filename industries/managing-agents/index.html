Common Problems

What Managing Agent Clients Come to Us With

Contractors Not Performing

Service contractors under-delivering, not attending on time, or producing poor quality work — leaving you to manage the complaints.

Compliance Certificates Not Tracked Centrally

Certificates scattered across different contractors, inboxes and files — no single record of compliance status across the managed portfolio.

Reactive Repairs Taking Too Long

Occupiers waiting days for a reactive repair response — affecting your relationship with the client and the occupiers.

No Monthly FM Reporting to Show Clients

Clients asking what has been done and what the FM spend looks like — with no structured reporting to give them.

FM Resource Stretched Across Too Many Sites

Your team managing too many sites with too little capacity — FM coordination falling through the gaps.

Difficult Contractor Relationships

Longstanding contractor arrangements that have become comfortable rather than value-driven — with no leverage to improve them.
