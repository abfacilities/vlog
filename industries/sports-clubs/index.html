Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Sports Clubs

Public Safety Obligations

Any facility open to the public — a cricket ground, football club, sports hall or bowling green — carries fire safety, electrical and structural safety obligations that must be managed formally.

Aging Clubhouse Infrastructure

Many sports club buildings are decades old with ageing electrical, heating and roofing systems. Without planned maintenance, problems accumulate until they become expensive emergencies.

Volunteer Committee Limitations

Volunteer treasurers and committee members are rarely FM professionals. Statutory obligations and planned maintenance can be overlooked simply because no one knows they exist.

Seasonal Pressures

Sports facilities often have concentrated use periods. Maintenance failures during the season are costly — both financially and reputationally. Off-season periods should be used for planned works.

Alcohol Licensing and Premises Compliance

Clubhouses with bar facilities have additional licensing and premises compliance obligations that sit alongside standard FM requirements.

Insurance and Governance

Sports clubs with employees, hireable facilities or public events need appropriate insurance, risk assessments and premises documentation — often required by governing bodies and funders.

FAQs

Common Questions

We're a members' club — do statutory obligations still apply?

Yes. Fire safety, electrical safety and other statutory requirements apply to any building used by the public, regardless of membership structure or charitable status.

How do we manage maintenance around the playing season?

We build your PPM schedule around the season — with major works planned for the closed season and reactive jobs coordinated quickly to avoid fixture cancellations.

Can you help us with our governing body requirements?

We can provide documentation and compliance records in formats suitable for submission to sporting governing bodies and funders.
