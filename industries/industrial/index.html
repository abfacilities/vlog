Common Problems

What Industrial Clients Come to Us With

No Planned Maintenance at All

Everything is reactive — breakdowns, emergency repairs, crisis management. The cost and disruption add up over time.

LOLER & Pressure Vessel Obligations Missed

Lifting equipment, overhead cranes and pressure vessels all require thorough examinations. Easy to overlook without a dedicated FM function.

Water Hygiene Not Managed

L8 risk assessments not updated, temperature monitoring not in place — a serious compliance and liability issue in industrial settings.

Gas & Electrical Compliance Lapsed

CGAS and EICR certificates out of date — often not discovered until a renewal, insurance check or inspection raises it.

Contractors Invoicing Without Oversight

Reactive maintenance being carried out without authorisation, documented scope or cost control.

FM Left with the Production Team

Building maintenance falling to operations managers who have neither the time nor the expertise to manage it properly.
