Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Growing Businesses

No Dedicated FM Function

Most SMEs don't have a facilities manager. Premises responsibilities fall to office managers, operations directors or the owner — none of whom have the capacity or expertise to manage FM properly.

Compliance Obligations Often Unknown

Many SME owners are unaware of their statutory premises obligations — fire risk assessments, electrical inspections, gas safety — until an inspection or incident reveals a gap.

Reactive Maintenance Costs

Without planned maintenance, everything becomes reactive. Reactive repairs cost more than planned maintenance and cause more disruption to business operations.

Contractor Management

SMEs often use the same contractors for everything — or struggle to find reliable, reasonably priced tradespeople. Without a managed contractor network, quality and pricing are inconsistent.

Lease Obligations

Many SMEs occupy leased premises without fully understanding which maintenance obligations fall to them as tenants. Missed obligations can result in landlord disputes and dilapidation claims at lease end.

Growth and Multi-Site Complexity

As businesses grow and open additional premises, the FM challenge multiplies. Without a scalable FM approach, maintaining consistent standards across multiple sites becomes very difficult.

FAQs

Common Questions

What statutory obligations does my business premises have?

At minimum: fire risk assessment (reviewed annually or after changes to the building), EICR (every 5 years for most commercial buildings), gas safety certificate if gas is present, and legionella risk assessment. Additional requirements depend on your industry and premises type.

We only have one small office — do we need FM support?

Even single-site businesses have statutory obligations. Our Essential package provides cost-effective compliance management and contractor coordination for smaller premises.

Can you help us manage our lease obligations?

Yes — we can review your lease to identify your maintenance obligations as a tenant and ensure you're meeting them — protecting you from landlord disputes and dilapidation claims.
