Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Housing Associations

Awaab's Law Compliance

From 2025, registered providers must investigate damp and mould reports within 14 days and begin remediation within 7 days where a health hazard is identified. FM systems must support this timeline.

Communal Area Compliance

Fire doors, emergency lighting, communal heating systems and lifts in communal areas carry statutory inspection and maintenance requirements — often across a large and geographically dispersed portfolio.

Contractor Quality and Performance

Housing associations frequently struggle with contractor performance — missed appointments, poor quality work and inadequate record-keeping. Structured contractor management improves outcomes and protects the organisation.

Resident Experience

Poor maintenance responsiveness damages resident satisfaction scores and creates regulatory risk. Visible, well-managed maintenance activity improves resident confidence and reduces formal complaints.

Regulatory Scrutiny

The Regulator of Social Housing and Housing Ombudsman are increasingly scrutinising how providers manage their stock. Clear maintenance records and compliance documentation are essential.

Multi-Site Portfolio Management

Managing maintenance and compliance across a large portfolio of properties — in multiple locations, with different ages and construction types — requires systematic FM oversight.

How does Awaab's Law affect FM management?

Awaab's Law requires registered providers to investigate damp and mould reports within 14 days and begin remediation within a further 7 days where a health hazard is present. FM systems must support this — with clear logging, response coordination and documentation.
