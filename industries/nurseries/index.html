Understanding the Sector

Premises Challenges for Nurseries & Early Years Settings

EYFS Environment Requirements

Early years settings must maintain safe, appropriate environments. Premises failures — broken equipment, temperature issues, pest problems — can affect Ofsted registration and lead to closures.

Contractor Safety on Site

Any maintenance contractor working in a setting with children present requires careful management. Nursery owners need assurance that all contractors are vetted, supervised and safe to be on site.

Gas, Electrical and Water Safety

Annual gas safety certificates, periodic electrical inspections and legionella risk assessments are statutory requirements. Missing renewals creates both a legal and insurance risk.

Limited Premises Management Capacity

Most nursery managers are focused on children's care and education — not premises administration. FM support allows them to focus on their primary role with confidence that the building is properly managed.

Landlord vs Occupier Obligations

Many nurseries occupy leased premises. Understanding which maintenance obligations fall to the landlord and which to the occupier — and ensuring both sides fulfil them — requires FM expertise.

Reactive Maintenance Impact

A boiler failure, broken lock or plumbing problem in a nursery can force closure. Responsive reactive maintenance coordination minimises disruption and protects the business.

FAQs

Common Questions

What statutory inspections does a nursery need?

At minimum: annual gas safety certificate (if gas is present), periodic EICR (typically every 5 years), annual fire risk assessment review, legionella risk assessment, and emergency lighting checks. PAT testing for electrical equipment is also good practice. We track all of these for you.

Can contractors attend while children are present?

We coordinate contractor visits to take place outside session times where possible, or ensure appropriate supervision and safeguarding arrangements are in place where unavoidable.

Do you work with leased premises?

Yes — we can review your lease to help identify maintenance obligations and liaise with landlords on their responsibilities where needed.
