Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Independent Restaurants

Kitchen Extraction Fire Safety

Commercial kitchen extraction systems must be professionally cleaned at regular intervals as a fire safety requirement and to maintain insurance validity. Many operators are unaware of their obligations until a fire or insurance claim.

Gas Safety in Commercial Kitchens

Commercial gas equipment requires annual safety checks by Gas Safe registered engineers. With multiple appliances in a typical kitchen, this can be complex to manage consistently.

Environmental Health Compliance

Environmental health officers inspect restaurant premises against food hygiene and premises safety standards. Maintenance failures — drainage, pest control, structural issues — directly affect ratings and can result in closure notices.

Refrigeration and Cold Chain

Commercial refrigeration failures can destroy stock and trigger food safety incidents. Planned maintenance and fast reactive response are both essential.

Landlord vs Tenant Obligations

Many restaurant operators lease their premises. Understanding which maintenance obligations fall to the landlord and which to the tenant — and ensuring the right party acts — requires FM expertise.

Downtime Cost

Every hour a restaurant is closed costs revenue. Fast reactive maintenance coordination and planned preventative maintenance both reduce the risk of unexpected closures.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often does kitchen extraction need cleaning?

Frequency depends on cooking volume and type — typically quarterly for high-volume kitchens and every 6 months for lower-volume operations. Your insurer's policy schedule will specify the required frequency. We coordinate this for you.

What statutory inspections does a restaurant require?

Annual gas safety certificate, periodic EICR, annual fire risk assessment review, legionella risk assessment (where applicable), emergency lighting tests and fire detection servicing. Kitchen extract cleaning is a separate insurance and fire safety requirement.

Can you help us prepare for an EHO inspection?

Yes — we review your premises maintenance records and documentation to identify gaps that could affect your rating, and help you address them before an inspection.
