Understanding the Sector

FM Challenges for Gyms & Leisure Facilities

Equipment Safety and Maintenance

Commercial fitness equipment requires regular servicing and inspection. Failures can cause member injuries — with significant liability implications if maintenance records are inadequate.

HVAC and Air Quality

Gyms require effective ventilation to manage air quality, humidity and temperature. HVAC system failures affect member experience and can create health risks in hot, humid environments.

Water Safety and Legionella

Shower facilities, pools and spa areas in leisure facilities are classified as high-risk for legionella. Water temperature monitoring, risk assessments and treatment programmes are statutory requirements.

High Footfall Building Wear

Gyms and leisure facilities experience significant daily footfall — accelerating wear on flooring, changing rooms, showers and common areas. Planned maintenance prevents deterioration and member complaints.

Member Safety and Liability

Any accident or injury in a gym that relates to building or equipment maintenance failures creates significant liability exposure. Documented maintenance programmes are essential protection.

Operational Hours Constraints

24/7 or extended-hours facilities have limited windows for maintenance. Coordinating contractors around opening hours without disrupting members requires careful FM planning.

FAQs

Common Questions

What statutory inspections does a gym require?

Fire risk assessment, EICR, gas safety (if applicable), legionella risk assessments and water temperature monitoring, emergency lighting tests, fire detection servicing. Pool facilities have additional water treatment and safety requirements.

Who is responsible for equipment safety checks?

Gym equipment manufacturers publish servicing schedules. Operators are responsible for ensuring these are followed. We coordinate equipment maintenance contracts with approved service providers.

How do you manage maintenance in a 24/7 facility?

We plan all maintenance activity around your lowest footfall periods and coordinate contractor access to avoid disrupting members. Reactive repairs are coordinated quickly to minimise downtime.
