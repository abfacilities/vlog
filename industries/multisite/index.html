FM quality varying site to site — some locations well-managed, others reactive, with no consistent baseline across the estate.

Different Contractors at Every Site

No consolidation across locations — paying more, getting less consistency and spending management time on contractor coordination at every site.

Compliance status different at every location — no single picture of where the whole estate stands and what needs attention.

Central team managing too many sites, too many contractors and too many issues — FM taking time away from core business operations.
