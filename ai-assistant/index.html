How often does an EICR need to be done on a commercial premises?
FM
For most commercial premises, an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is recommended every 5 years, though the frequency can vary based on the type of premises and its risk level.

Higher-risk environments — such as industrial sites, premises with wet processes, or older buildings — may need testing more frequently, sometimes every 1–3 years.

The EICR must be carried out by a qualified electrician or electrical engineer registered with a competent person scheme such as NICEIC or NAPIT. Any Category 1 (C1) or Category 2 (C2) remedials identified should be addressed promptly.
You
What's the difference between C1 and C2 remedials?
FM
C1 — Danger present: Immediate risk of injury. Requires urgent action — the circuit or equipment should be isolated immediately until repaired.

C2 — Potentially dangerous: Not immediately dangerous, but could become so. Should be addressed as soon as practicable — typically within 28 days.

There is also C3 (Improvement recommended), which is advisory rather than a requirement.
