Privacy Policy

Who we are

AB Facilities Consultants ("we", "us") is a facilities management consultancy based in Liverpool, providing services across the North West of England. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect any personal data you share with us via this website.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us directly, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, organisation and the details of your enquiry.

How we use it

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to provide the services you've asked about. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry and client data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or request deletion of the personal data we hold about you. To make a request, email contact@abfacilities.com.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies required for basic functionality. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@abfacilities.com.

This policy is provided as general guidance and should be reviewed by a qualified adviser to ensure full compliance with UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 requirements specific to your organisation.
