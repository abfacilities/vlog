We'll keep this page updated as deadlines firm up. Martyn's Law (Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025) Who it affects: Schools, academies, community centres, sports clubs and any venue that can reasonably expect 200+ people at once. Key date: Enforceable from spring 2027 (24-month implementation period from April 2025 commencement; Home Office published final statutory guidance April 2026). What's required: Standard-tier venues need documented procedures for an attack (invacuation, lockdown, evacuation) and trained staff — not physical security infrastructure. Read our full guide → Awaab's Law Phase 2 Who it affects: Housing associations, local authorities and registered social housing providers. Key date: 30 November 2026. What's required: Fixed response deadlines extend beyond damp and mould to excess cold/heat, falls, structural collapse, fire/explosions, electrical hazards, and hygiene/food safety. Asbestos and carbon monoxide follow in 2027. Read our full guide → Crime and Policing Act 2026 — safeguarding changes Who it affects: Schools and nurseries. Key date: From 1 September 2026 (2026/27 academic year). What's required: Removes the long-standing supervision exemption from the definition of regulated activity, affecting how schools manage visitors and site access. Read our full guide → Building Safety Act 2022 / Building Safety Regulator oversight Who it affects: Higher-risk buildings and, increasingly, wider commercial and public-sector estates as BSR oversight extends. What's changing: Updated fire door standards and a continuing shift from paper-based to digital compliance record-keeping. Read our full guide → BS 8214:2026 — new fire door code of practice Who it affects: Building owners and Responsible Persons maintaining fire doors of any type — timber, steel, glazed or composite. Key date: BS 8214:2016 was withdrawn on 20 March 2026. What's required: Wider scope covering all fire door types, and a shift from prescriptive rules to evidence-based verification — relevant to your Golden Thread documentation. Read our full guide → Digital Waste Tracking Who it affects: Operators of permitted or licensed waste sites first; every organisation with a waste contractor soon after. Key date: Mandatory from 1 October 2026 (Phase 1); waste carriers, brokers and exporters follow from October 2027. What's required: Waste movements recorded electronically rather than on paper transfer notes. Read our full guide → The Hillsborough Law (Public Office (Accountability) Bill) Who it affects: Local authorities, academy trusts and other public bodies — still progressing through Parliament, not yet in force. What's proposed: A statutory duty of candour requiring proactive, honest disclosure to inquiries and inquests, rather than waiting to be asked. Read our full guide → Fire Safety (Residential Evacuation Plans) Regulations 2025 Who it affects: Owners and managing agents of high-rise and certain other residential buildings — relevant if you manage or advise on residential blocks alongside your commercial sites. Key date: In force from 6 April 2026. What's required: Person-centred fire risk assessments identifying residents who'd need help evacuating, with tailored evacuation arrangements. Read our full guide → Not sure which of these apply to your site?
