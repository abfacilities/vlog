2026/27 Compliance Changes at a Glance

A running summary of the facilities-relevant regulatory changes landing across the North West over the next 18 months. We'll keep this page updated as deadlines firm up.

Martyn's Law (Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025)

Who it affects: Schools, academies, community centres, sports clubs and any venue that can reasonably expect 200+ people at once.
Key date: Enforceable from spring 2027 (24-month implementation period from April 2025 commencement; Home Office published final statutory guidance April 2026).
What's required: Standard-tier venues need documented procedures for an attack (invacuation, lockdown, evacuation) and trained staff — not physical security infrastructure.
Read our full guide →

Awaab's Law Phase 2

Who it affects: Housing associations, local authorities and registered social housing providers.
Key date: 30 November 2026.
What's required: Fixed response deadlines extend beyond damp and mould to excess cold/heat, falls, structural collapse, fire/explosions, electrical hazards, and hygiene/food safety. Asbestos and carbon monoxide follow in 2027.
Read our full guide →

Crime and Policing Act 2026 — safeguarding changes

Who it affects: Schools and nurseries.
Key date: From 1 September 2026 (2026/27 academic year).
What's required: Removes the long-standing supervision exemption from the definition of regulated activity, affecting how schools manage visitors and site access.
Read our full guide →

Building Safety Act 2022 / Building Safety Regulator oversight

Who it affects: Higher-risk buildings and, increasingly, wider commercial and public-sector estates as BSR oversight extends.
What's changing: Updated fire door standards and a continuing shift from paper-based to digital compliance record-keeping.
