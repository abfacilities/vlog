Facilities support that fits your organisation

Choose the level of support your site needs — from a monthly review of issues and priorities to ongoing help coordinating maintenance, contractors and compliance activities.

Packages start at £650 per month. Before you commit, we’ll agree the scope, included time and responsibilities so you know exactly what to expect.

There are three ways to work with us: a monthly package for recurring support on a defined site and workload, one-off work for a specific assessment, review or project, or Interim FM for temporary day-rate cover where volume and responsibilities are agreed upfront.

Commercial details

Commitment & cancellation

Monthly packages have an initial three-month commitment, then continue on a rolling monthly basis. Cancel by email with 30 days’ notice once the initial term is complete. Any partial final month is charged proportionately.

Travel

Travel for scheduled package visits is included for sites within 25 road miles of our Bootle base. Any additional travel, parking or tolls are agreed in writing before the engagement begins.

One-off projects and Bespoke engagements have their terms confirmed in the written proposal.

How to choose

Most ongoing support starts with Core or Plus for a single site. Enhanced suits sites that need more contractor coordination and remote support between visits. Bespoke covers complex or multi-site work priced against an agreed workload. If you're not sure where you sit, an Audit is a good starting point to establish what's actually needed.

Every quote is confirmed in writing before work begins, and reviewed against agreed KPIs so the level of support can flex as your needs change.
