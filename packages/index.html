How to choose

Most engagements start with an Audit or Cost & Contract Review to establish what's actually needed. From there, ongoing support usually moves to a Fractional or Managed FM retainer — a managed outcome rather than a day-rate. Interim FM suits short-notice, full-time cover; Multi-site FM suits portfolios and trusts with several locations.

Every quote is confirmed in writing before work begins, and reviewed against agreed KPIs so the level of support can flex as your needs change. If you're not sure which one fits, tell us what you need and we'll recommend the right level.
