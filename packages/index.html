FM management fees

Choose the level of coordinated FM support your site needs. The management fee covers AB's time to plan, coordinate and report. Specialist service costs — cleaning, maintenance contractors, compliance testing — are always quoted separately.

FM management fees start at £695/month. Before you commit, we agree the scope, included time and responsibilities in writing, so you know exactly what to expect.

There are three ways to work with us: a monthly FM management contract for recurring coordinated support, specialist and project work for a specific assessment or review, or Interim FM for temporary day-rate cover where volume and responsibilities are agreed upfront.

Commercial details

Commitment & cancellation

Monthly packages have an initial three-month commitment, then continue on a rolling monthly basis. You may give 30 days’ notice by email at any time, provided cancellation takes effect no earlier than the end of the initial three-month term. Any partial final month is charged proportionately.

Travel

Travel for scheduled package visits is included for sites within 25 road miles of our Bootle base. Any additional travel, parking or tolls are agreed in writing before the engagement begins.

VAT

AB Facilities Consultants is not VAT-registered. Prices shown are the full amount payable — no VAT is added.

One-off projects and bespoke engagements have their terms confirmed in the written proposal.

How to choose

Most ongoing support starts with Essential or Professional for a single site. Enterprise suits sites that need full contractor coordination and dedicated remote support between visits. Multi-Site covers complex or multi-site work priced against an agreed portfolio. If you're not sure where you sit, a Compliance Audit is a good starting point to establish what's actually needed.

Every quote is confirmed in writing before work begins, and reviewed against agreed KPIs so the level of support can flex as your needs change.

Common questions

What happens after I enquire?

We start with a short call about your site and priorities, then agree a written scope and proposal before anything is signed. After that comes a site review and a prioritised action plan, followed by the agreed ongoing support.

How long is the commitment?

Monthly packages have an initial three-month commitment, then continue on a rolling monthly basis. You can give 30 days' notice by email, as long as cancellation takes effect no earlier than the end of the initial three months.

What if I need more than my package includes?

Anything beyond the included allowance, such as an extra site visit or an unplanned contractor issue, is scoped and quoted before it proceeds. We never bill for extra work without agreeing it with you first.

Are VAT and travel extra?

AB Facilities Consultants is not VAT-registered, so the prices shown are the full amount payable. Travel for scheduled package visits is included for sites within 25 road miles of our Bootle base.

Can I start with a one-off audit instead?

Yes. A Compliance Audit (£495–£750 per site) is the usual starting point and gives you a prioritised written report before you decide on ongoing support.

How quickly will you reply?

Messages sent through the contact form are usually answered within two working days.
