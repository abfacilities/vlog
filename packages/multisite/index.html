Priced on scope, not a fixed rate

Multi-Site is not a standard tier — it's a bespoke engagement priced against the number of sites, complexity, included responsibilities and reporting structure you need. The £5,000/month floor reflects the minimum viable scope for a genuine portfolio engagement. The first step is a scoping conversation.

What's typically included

  • Coverage across multiple sites — scope, frequency and responsibilities agreed per portfolio
  • Portfolio compliance management — a single picture of compliance status across all sites
  • Cross-site contractor panels — consistent approved supplier lists across the estate
  • Bespoke reporting structure — tailored to what your board, trustees or management team need to see
  • Dedicated account management — one point of contact across the entire portfolio
  • Procurement and tendering across the estate
  • Budget tracking and capital planning support

Who this is for

Multi-Site is built for three types of client: multi-academy trusts managing facilities across five or more schools; commercial property companies managing a portfolio of offices, retail units or industrial premises; and care or housing providers operating multiple registered sites. The common thread is a portfolio where consistent standards, shared contractor relationships and a single oversight function create real efficiency.

How the scoping process works

Scoping conversation

A free call covering the number of sites, current arrangements, compliance priorities and what oversight you actually need.

Written proposal

A fixed-price proposal detailing scope, included time, reporting cadence, responsibilities and what happens if needs change.

Portfolio baseline

A structured review across all sites in month one, producing a compliance and maintenance picture for the whole estate.

Ongoing portfolio management

Regular site visits, contractor coordination, monthly portfolio reporting and board-ready summaries as required.

Not sure if you need Multi-Site?

If you're managing two or three sites, Enterprise may cover it. For four or more sites with consistent FM needs, Multi-Site typically delivers better value. We'll tell you which fits in the scoping call.
