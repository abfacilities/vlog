What's included

  • Monthly site review visit (half day on site)
  • Written priority report after each visit — compliance issues, maintenance priorities and recommendations
  • Compliance status overview — identifying any statutory obligations that need attention
  • Email support between visits for follow-up queries
  • Direct access to a qualified FM consultant (not a call centre)

Who this is for

Organisations running a single site that want a professional eye on facilities and compliance each month — without employing an FM. Typical clients on Essential include small care homes, community venues, schools seeking a first FM review, and SME office occupiers.

How it works

Month 1 — baseline

We walk the site together, establish the current picture and produce a prioritised compliance and maintenance report.

Ongoing — monthly rhythm

A site visit each month, a written report within five working days, and email support in between.

Transparent scope

All included time and responsibilities are agreed in writing before we start, so there are no surprises.

Easy to scale

As your site grows or needs more support, upgrade to Professional at any point with no penalty.

What it doesn't include

Essential is a review-and-advise service. It doesn't include contractor liaison or coordination, procurement, project management, or more than minimal follow-up between visits. For those, Professional is the right fit.
