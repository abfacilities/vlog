Who this is for

Enterprise is for single sites — or small two-to-three-site organisations — where facilities management is genuinely complex: a care home with a full maintenance programme, a school with significant contractor relationships, a commercial building with multiple tenants or a property where there's been FM cover in the past and the need is well-established.

How it works

Dedicated consultant

The same person visits every time, builds relationships with your contractors and knows your site inside out.

Three visits per month

Roughly weekly presence on site — enough to maintain real momentum on works and compliance.

Procurement and tendering

We manage the market for your key contracts — cleaning, maintenance, grounds — and advise on switching where it makes sense.

Budget oversight

Monthly reporting against your facilities budget, with forecasting for planned works and renewal decisions.

When to consider Multi-Site

If you're managing more than two or three sites — or a portfolio where the total scope significantly exceeds one dedicated consultant's time — Multi-Site is built for that. We'll scope it honestly against what you actually need.
