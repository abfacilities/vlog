Compliance Audit Detail

What a Compliance Audit Covers

Our compliance audits give you a clear picture of where your statutory compliance stands — across every discipline that affects your building. The written report tells you what's in order, what needs attention and in what priority.

Electrical

EICR currency, distribution board condition, emergency lighting test records and outstanding remedials.

Fire Safety

Fire alarm, emergency lighting, suppression, extinguishers and fire risk assessment — currency and remedials.

Water Hygiene (L8)

Legionella risk assessment, written scheme, temperature monitoring records and system documentation.

Gas

CP17 / gas safety certificate currency, boiler service records and gas system documentation.

HVAC & F-Gas

Air conditioning and refrigeration service records, F-Gas registration and leak testing documentation.

LOLER

Lifting equipment thorough examination certificates — currency and outstanding remedials for all lifting equipment.
