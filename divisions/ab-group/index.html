AB Group

AB Group is the future parent holding identity for all AB divisions — the umbrella above AB Facilities Consultants, AB Cleaning, AB Technical and AB Property once the full group structure is established.

AB Group is not currently trading.

The active business is AB Facilities Consultants. All current service enquiries, quotes and fault reports should be directed there. AB Group is a planned future parent identity and does not currently operate or hold contracts.

Group Structure

The AB Group Vision

As the AB Facilities division structure matures, AB Group will become the parent holding company — bringing together the FM consultancy, cleaning, technical and property arms under a single group identity. This model allows each division to focus on its specialism while sharing common infrastructure, standards and brand.
