Group Structure

Our Divisions

The AB Group brings together five identities — a group holding company and four specialist divisions delivering FM consultancy, cleaning, technical services and property management across the North West.

AB Group is the group holding identity. AB Facilities Consultants is the established FM consultancy and the principal trading business. AB Cleaning, AB Technical and AB Property are the specialist service divisions. All enquiries can be directed to the relevant division or through AB Facilities Consultants.
