AB Technical

AB Technical is the specialist technical FM division of the AB Group — providing electrical, mechanical, HVAC and building fabric services to AB Facilities Consultants clients and directly contracted sites across the region.

About This Division

What AB Technical Provides

AB Technical gives the AB Group in-house technical capacity — reducing reliance on third-party contractors for routine technical work and improving response times for managed clients. Services cover the full range of building services typically required under an FM contract.

Electrical Services

EICR testing, remedial works, general electrical maintenance and emergency lighting — to BS7671 standards.

Mechanical & HVAC

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and plumbing — planned maintenance, L8 compliance and reactive repair.

Building Fabric

Planned and reactive building fabric maintenance — roofing, fabric repairs, joinery and general building works.
