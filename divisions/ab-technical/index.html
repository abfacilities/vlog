AB Technical

AB Technical will provide specialist technical FM services — electrical, mechanical, HVAC and building fabric — to support AB Facilities Consultants clients and standalone technical clients across the region.

This division is not yet trading.

AB Technical is currently in development. For any technical FM requirements, please contact AB Facilities Consultants — specialist contractors are currently managed and coordinated on behalf of clients through the FM management service.

About This Division

What AB Technical Will Provide

AB Technical will give the AB Group in-house technical capacity — reducing reliance on third-party contractors for routine technical work and improving response times for managed clients. Services will cover the full range of building services typically required under an FM contract.

Electrical Services

EICR testing, remedial works, general electrical maintenance and emergency lighting — to BS7671 standards.

Mechanical & HVAC

Heating, ventilation, air conditioning and plumbing — planned maintenance, L8 compliance and reactive repair.

Building Fabric

Planned and reactive building fabric maintenance — roofing, fabric repairs, joinery and general building works.
