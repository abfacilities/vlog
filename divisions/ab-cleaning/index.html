AB Cleaning

AB Cleaning will be the dedicated commercial cleaning arm of the AB Group — delivering managed cleaning services to AB Facilities Consultants clients and directly contracted sites across the North West.

This division is not yet trading.

AB Cleaning is currently in development. For any cleaning requirements or enquiries, please contact AB Facilities Consultants — cleaning is currently procured and coordinated on behalf of clients as part of the FM management service.

About This Division

What AB Cleaning Will Provide

When established, AB Cleaning will offer a managed commercial cleaning service — handling staffing, supervision, supplies and quality control so facilities managers and building owners don't have to manage it directly. Services will initially focus on clients already within the AB Facilities Consultants portfolio.

Office & Commercial Cleaning

Regular cleaning for office buildings, business parks and commercial premises — daily, weekly and periodic schedules.

Industrial & Warehouse

Cleaning for industrial and logistics sites — including welfare facilities, canteens and high-traffic areas.

Education & Care

Specialist cleaning for schools, nurseries and care settings — with safeguarding-aware supervision and appropriate products.

Need cleaning now?

Contact AB Facilities Consultants — we coordinate cleaning for managed clients and can advise on interim arrangements.
