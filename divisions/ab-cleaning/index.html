AB Cleaning

AB Cleaning is the dedicated commercial cleaning division of the AB Group — delivering managed cleaning services to AB Facilities Consultants clients and directly contracted sites across the North West.

About This Division

What AB Cleaning Provides

AB Cleaning offers a managed commercial cleaning service — handling staffing, supervision, supplies and quality control so facilities managers and building owners don't have to manage it directly. Services are initially focused on clients within the AB Facilities Consultants portfolio, with direct contracts available for suitable sites.

Office & Commercial Cleaning

Regular cleaning for office buildings, business parks and commercial premises — daily, weekly and periodic schedules.

Industrial & Warehouse

Cleaning for industrial and logistics sites — including welfare facilities, canteens and high-traffic areas.

Education & Care

Specialist cleaning for schools, nurseries and care settings — with safeguarding-aware supervision and appropriate products.
