About This Division

What AB Property Will Provide

AB Property will bring together the property-related services that sit alongside FM consultancy — giving landlords, managing agents and property companies a dedicated point of contact for the management and compliance of their property portfolios.

Property Management

Day-to-day management of commercial and mixed-use properties — planned maintenance, compliance, contractor oversight and tenant liaison.

Vacant Property Inspections

Scheduled inspections of vacant properties for insurance compliance, security monitoring and condition reporting.

Landlord Compliance

Ensuring commercial and residential landlords meet their statutory obligations — EICR, gas safety, fire risk assessment, asbestos and legionella.
